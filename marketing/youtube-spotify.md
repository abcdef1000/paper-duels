# YouTube and Spotify Ads

Game URL: https://abcdef1000.github.io/paper-duels/

**Bottom line:** both platforms make it hard to reach under-18 students directly, by design. Use **YouTube** with *content* targeting (physics revision channels, PSLE topics) and **Spotify** aimed at **PSLE parents**. Both are lower priority than Reddit and organic channels. ### Targeting setup

| Setting | Student campaign (H2 Physics) | Parent campaign (PSLE) |
|---|---|---|
| Location | Singapore | Singapore |
| Age | Leave "Unknown" **on** (that's where teens fall). Don't try to exclude adults to get at teens. | 35–54, Parental status: Parent |
| Targeting type | **Content**: placements (specific A-level/physics revision channels and videos), topics (Education > Science), keywords ("a level physics", "h2 physics", "paper 1 mcq") | Topics: Education / Primary school; keywords "PSLE science", "PSLE maths" |
| Format | Shorts plus skippable in-stream | Skippable in-stream |
| Budget | S$5/day test, 10–14 days | S$5/day, Jul–Sep only |

**Expected cost:** S$70 for 14 days at S$5/day, which gives roughly **1,400–2,300 skippable views** at S$0.03–0.05 CPV, or about 4k–9k Shorts impressions at S$8–18 CPM. Agency estimates. Expect worse at a S$5/day scale and in Q4.

**Don't** mark ads or targeting as "made for kids" content (no personalisation, and very limited inventory).

## Spotify (Ad Studio / Spotify Ads Manager)

### Key facts

| Topic | Finding | Source and date |
|---|---|---|
| Self-serve in Singapore? | **Yes.** "All advertisers in Singapore can use our self-serve ad channel." | Spotify Advertising SG launch page (undated; the page shows © 2026) |
| Minimum spend | **S$250** ("budget minimum is 250 SGD" / "Starting at just S$250 per ad set"). One SG agency blog (Jun 2026) says S$350, which conflicts with Spotify's figure. Check at sign-up. | Spotify SG pages; marketingagency.sg |
| Pricing model | "Cost per ad served". You pay for delivered impressions (CPM-like). **No official SG CPM published.** | Spotify SG self-serve page |
| Formats | Audio and video ads in self-serve. Podcast ads are self-serve in some markets (announced on the Spotify CA site). **Singapore podcast self-serve not confirmed.** | Spotify Advertising pages |
| Targeting | Location, device, moment, format; also genre, interests and context. Third-party sources add age, gender and podcast category. | Spotify SG launch page; third-party guides |
| Free voiceover | Ad Studio offers free voiceover production for audio ads | Spotify SG launch page |
| Under-18s | **No official information found** on targeting 13–17-year-olds. Aim at adults and parents. | n/a |

### Targeting setup
- **Audience:** Singapore, ages 30–55 (parents) for the PSLE script, Jul–Sep. A second ad set for 18–24 (JC leavers, uni students who tutor) is optional.
- **Context:** "Study / focus" playlists and moments (if offered) for the student script.
- **Budget:** one ad set at the S$250 minimum, about 2–3 weeks.
- **Expected outcome:** awareness only. Audio is hard to attribute. Say a short, memorable URL out loud, or ask listeners to search "Paper One Duel".
